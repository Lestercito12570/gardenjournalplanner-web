Before you dig in

A few useful answers.

What is Garden Journal: Planner?

It’s an app for organizing garden beds, plants, planting plans, recurring care tasks, journal entries, and photos. It brings your planning and garden records together.

Can I use it in my browser?

Yes. Select “Open App” to use the existing web app at gardening.gardenshedclay.com. This website introduces the product; your garden records live in the app.

Is it just for vegetable gardens?

No. You can keep records for vegetables, flowers, and herbs, and organize the garden around what you grow.

Where can I see subscription details?

Open the app to review the current subscription options and any available trial before subscribing.

Do I need to move my existing account?

No. Existing users can continue using the same app address and account. This new website provides another way to find and open the app.
