About the gardener

Hello, I’m Lester.

I’m a self-taught gardener. Every season brings something new to learn. I created Garden Journal: Planner to bring garden plans, plant records, and the little discoveries we make along the way into one place.

Whether you’re growing vegetables, flowers, herbs, or a little of everything, I hope it helps you enjoy your garden and carry what you learn into the next season.

Before you dig in

A few useful answers.

What is Garden Journal: Planner?

It’s an app for organizing garden beds, plants, planting plans, recurring care tasks, journal entries, and photos. It brings your planning and garden records together.

Can I use it in my browser?

Yes. Select “Open App” to sign in and use Garden Journal: Planner in your browser.

Is it just for vegetable gardens?

No. You can keep records for vegetables, flowers, and herbs, and organize the garden around what you grow.

Where can I see subscription details?

Web subscriptions are $1.99 USD per month or $19.99 USD per year. For iPhone subscriptions, check the current price in the App Store before subscribing.

Is Garden Journal: Planner available for iPhone?

Yes. Download Garden Journal: Planner on the App Store to use it on your iPhone.

Can I choose metric or imperial measurements?

Yes. In the app’s settings, choose metric (meters and centimeters) or imperial (feet and inches) for supported garden measurements.

Can I set my hardiness zone and hemisphere?

Yes. Settings include USDA, Canadian, UK (RHS), European, Japanese, Australian, and New Zealand zone systems. Choose your zone and the Northern or Southern Hemisphere to adjust seasonal guidance. You can also save your average first and last frost dates.

Which languages are available?

The website and app offer English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Greek, and Scottish Gaelic. Choose the website language above; set the app language separately in its settings. Screenshots and external pages may remain in their original language.
